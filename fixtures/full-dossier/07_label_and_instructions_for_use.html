Instructions for Use

OxiTrack Fingertip Pulse Oximeter, model OT-500.

Intended use: Spot-check measurement of functional oxygen saturation (SpO2) and pulse rate in adults and paediatric patients in hospitals, clinics and the home.

Specifications

SpO2 measurement range: 70% to 100%

SpO2 accuracy: ±3% (70% to 100%)

Pulse rate range: 30 to 240 bpm

Batteries: two AAA 1.5 V alkaline batteries

Warnings

Do not use on neonates. Readings may be inaccurate with nail polish, low perfusion or movement.
