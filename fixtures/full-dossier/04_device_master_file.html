1.0 Executive Summary

The OxiTrack OT-500 is a battery-powered fingertip pulse oximeter for spot-check measurement of functional oxygen saturation and pulse rate. It is a Class C medical device.

2.1 Device Description

Device name: OxiTrack Fingertip Pulse Oximeter

Generic name: Pulse oximeter

Model number: OT-500

Manufacturer: Example Biomedical Pvt Ltd

Materials of construction: polycarbonate housing; medical-grade silicone finger pad; LED emitter and photodiode sensor.

Intended use: Spot-check, non-invasive measurement of functional oxygen saturation (SpO2) and pulse rate in adults in hospitals, clinics and the home.

Indications: patients requiring spot-checks of oxygen saturation. Contraindications: not for continuous monitoring or for use on neonates.

Warnings: readings may be inaccurate with low perfusion, nail polish or excessive movement.

2.2 Product Specification

SpO2 measurement range: 70% to 100%

SpO2 accuracy: ±2% (70% to 100%)

Pulse rate range: 30 to 240 bpm

Power supply: two AAA 1.5 V alkaline batteries

Display: OLED

6.0 Risk analysis and control summary

Risk analysis was performed per ISO 14971:2019 using FMEA. Identified hazards and controls are summarised in the risk management report EB-RMR-001. All residual risks were evaluated as acceptable and the overall residual risk is acceptable.

7.1 Verification and validation

Electrical safety and essential performance: tested to IEC 60601-1 and ISO 80601-2-61 (report EB-TR-014, pass). SpO2 accuracy validated by a controlled desaturation study (ARMS 1.8%).

7.2 Biocompatibility

Materials in direct contact with the patient: medical-grade silicone finger pad (surface contact with intact skin, limited duration) and polycarbonate housing.

Biocompatibility testing per ISO 10993-1, ISO 10993-5 (cytotoxicity) and ISO 10993-10 (sensitisation and irritation) was performed on the finished device. Result: no cytotoxicity, sensitisation or irritation (report EB-BIO-003).

7.3 Software

Embedded firmware version 2.1, software safety class B per IEC 62304; verification and validation report EB-SW-002.

7.4 Stability

Shelf life of 3 years supported by accelerated ageing study EB-STB-001.
